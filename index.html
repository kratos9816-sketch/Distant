Chapters of My Heart

Written Honestly, For You

Chapter 01

You Are My Whole World

In a world full of noise and people, you are the only one who truly matters to me. Having you in my life changed everything. Every dream, every priority, and every thought I carry revolves around you—I only have you, and no one else could ever take your place.

Chapter 02

The Thought of Losing You

The mere thought of a life without you stops me in my tracks. It hurts deeper than words can express. If things have felt chaotic or difficult, I want to listen, understand, and fix whatever we need to fix. Please don't walk away from us.

Chapter 03

Soulmates & Our Future

What we share isn't ordinary—we are soulmates. From the moment we connected, I saw my entire future with you. My intention has always been complete and unwavering: to stand by you, walk this road together, and build a marriage that lasts a lifetime.

Chapter 04

என் மனதின் ஆசை

என் வாழ்க்கையில் நீ மட்டும்தான் எனக்கு எல்லாமே. எந்த சூழ்நிலையிலும் உன்னை இழக்க நான் தயாராக இல்லை.

"கெஞ்சி கேட்கிறேன்... என்னை விட்டு மட்டும் போயிடாதே."
